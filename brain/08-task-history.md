## Decisions worth remembering
- **Pando the panda** is the About-page mascot; the human guide is *not* used on About. Home services walk uses the original
  cartoon avatar again (task 36), panda only in About/At-a-glance.
- **Tech stack**: v1 3D layers and v2 constellations were rejected; current = solar system (planets = categories, random orbits,
  hover → locked pop-up). Overlap of planets: options were (A) same average speed with modulation or (B) different speeds with
  slow-down behind slower planets; the owner has not chosen — mention at most once.
- **Industries**: radar (orbit) was replaced by a 3D low-poly city with a road and car; Home uses a straight infinite road with a
  bird's-eye/45° camera whose values the owner set via the tuner.
- **Why We3vision**: heavy 3D versions rejected; light "sticky check list + reasons pass by" (ReasonsStack) on classic pages,
  Bento on modern ones.
- **Process**: 3D floor path (ProcessFloor) for classic pages, ProcessRail for modern.
- **About timeline**: the horizontal roadmap was rejected and deleted; vertical timeline with top-view avatars walking is final.
  2027 office: open plan, micro/light, no carpet boxes, no patterns, not crowded (v3–v5).
- **Metaverse**: floor scene is a one-screen copy of the About office with 4 corners AR/VR/XR/MR; the zoom/cabin tour was rejected;
  device table is flat (earlier glow version "looked AI generated"); quiz keeps glass+glow. vrSpace and vrEmerge removed.
- **Brand page**: Bungee-like arch gallery (own SVG art placeholders until real client photos), brand board v5 (pinned, avatar walks the
  street), wordHero Archivo 900 slot-machine word in 7 languages. 4 sub-pages removed (38).
- **Careers**: single train journey across the page; ticket office first screen; Surat Junction last section holds the job list at
  left; resume upload to `Media`.
- **Avatar**: the owner's 3D chibi character sheet; PNG/traced-SVG versions replaced by the hand-built rigged vector (`avatar.tsx`).
- **Contact**: receptionist scene + 2-step guided flow replaces a plain form.
- **Colour theme picker** touches the whole site; admin excluded.
